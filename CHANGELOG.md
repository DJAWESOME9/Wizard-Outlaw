# Changelog

## 0.4.0 — Deeper roots, greater power

2026-11-17

- Expanded Wizard to 13 class skills, and Hexslinger and Debtmancer to 12 each, with new defensive branches and late-tree upgrades.
- Wizard gains mana reserves, regeneration, armor, mobility, mastery, and reload recovery. Hexslinger gains gun damage, reload wards, armor, mobility, and piercing hex rounds.
- Debtmancer gains regeneration, armor, mobility, healing reloads, blood-payment wards, and a once-per-run escape from a fatal hit.
- Every spell card and sigil gains mana-efficiency, mana-on-kill, and mastery branches. Existing ranks and saved progress are preserved.
- Trees open at readable zoom with fixed node positions; pan, zoom, or use FIT TREE to inspect the complete branching layout.

## 0.3.1 — Slower debt settlements

2026-11-17

- Debtmancer now restores health and mana every 15 kill credits instead of every kill.
- Rapid multikills earn diminishing credit within one-second bursts; summoned enemies give no credit.
- The Blood Ledger readout tracks settlement progress, and Debt Collector upgrades each payout.

## 0.3.0 — A path for every outlaw

2026-11-17

- Added dedicated Hexslinger and Debtmancer skill trees alongside the Wizard tree, with permanent class upgrades and larger card bandoliers.
- Open any class tree with its SKILL TREE button during setup; purchases enforce prerequisites, XP costs, and rank caps.
- Fixed skill boxes jumping on hover and purchase, improved node spacing, and made recentering fit the full tree on desktop and mobile.
- Class resource readouts now reflect upgraded hex rounds, mana recovery, blood costs, and spell power.

## 0.2.0 — Dispatches from the frontier

2026-11-17

- Added a What’s New popup and an in-game changelog you can reopen anytime.
- Update notices appear once per release in this browser, after you dismiss them.
- Reading release notes pauses your run and returns you to your previous screen.

## 0.1.0 — The frontier so far

Existing game baseline; earlier releases were not individually versioned.

- Three playable classes: Wizard, Hexslinger, and Debtmancer, with permanent grimoire progression and saved loadouts.
- Draw sigils, drag spell cards, or draw your revolver; sprint and dash with stamina.
- Build each run with stackable relics, dedicated Magic Missile upgrades, and revolver upgrades.
- Astral Stars accepts outline and crossing stars, with visible golden homing projectiles.
- Vortex recognizes clockwise and counterclockwise spirals across varied loop counts.
- Enemy health, pressure, speed, and damage ramp up after level 5. Desktop, touch, and fullscreen controls are supported.
